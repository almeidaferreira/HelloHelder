Shoe Poster Studio

Spin the shoe, pin a frame, edit the layout, export the poster. Changes save automatically in this browser.

Fit

Drag to rotate · scroll to resize the shoe · Ctrl or ⌘ + scroll to zoom the canvas

Pin a frame to keep this angle and glow state. Click a pinned frame to return to it.
Text

Upload a TTF, OTF, WOFF or WOFF2 file for the headline (bold) and another for the subline (light). One file is used for both. Fonts stay in your browser only.

Format and colour
Partner logos

The strip shows typographic stand-ins for the six partner marks. Add your real PNG or SVG logos and they replace the stand-ins, spaced evenly in the order you pick them.

Shoes

Click a shoe on the poster, or its chip, to select it. Model, palette, size, tilt and position edit the selected shoe. Shift + drag moves it. Up to 6 shoes.

3D shoe
Glow palette

Sneaker model: "Materials Variants Shoe" by Shopify, CC BY 4.0. Load your own OBJ to wrap the same glowing layers around it (beta, works best with a single closed mesh).

Reset all settings?

This clears the text, colours, format, shoes, pinned frames, uploaded logos and fonts saved in this browser, and starts again from the defaults. It cannot be undone.

Export poster

30 fps H.264 MP4, rendered frame by frame in your browser. Needs a recent Chrome, Edge or Safari.

Right-click or long-press the preview to save it. If the download button does nothing in this view, use that.
